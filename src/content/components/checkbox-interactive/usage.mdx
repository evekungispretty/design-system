Checkbox Interactive turns a [Checkbox](/components/checkbox) and its label into one large, tappable card. It suits multiple-choice prompts where each option is a full sentence — in the source file, a list of things a parent might be worried about.

Clicking anywhere on the row toggles the checkbox. The card signals selection with a red border and hover with a stronger shadow, so the choices feel like discrete objects rather than form rows.

It fills the width of its container. In Figma it is drawn at 472px wide.
