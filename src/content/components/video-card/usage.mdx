Video Card presents one video in a list or carousel. The thumbnail leads and a short description sits beneath.

The entire card is a single control. Give it an `href` to link to the video, or an `onClick` to open it in a player. On hover or keyboard focus, a dark scrim covers the thumbnail and a "觀看影片" (watch video) pill with an eye icon appears in the middle.

The play badge shown in the default state is part of the thumbnail image in Figma, not something the card draws. Supply thumbnails that include it if you want it.

The card fills its container's width. In Figma it is drawn at 346px.
