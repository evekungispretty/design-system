### Description

`#1A1A1A`, 13.5px regular. Figma sets this one line in Noto Sans TC rather than the LINE Seed family used elsewhere in the card set.

### Hover overlay

A 60% black scrim over the thumbnail only. In the middle sits a pill with a 24px eye icon and the label in `text/body-sm-bold` (step -1, weight 700), white, with `elevation/1`-style drop shadow, `space/xs` horizontal and `space/2xs` vertical padding.

The overlay fades in over 150ms and appears on both hover and keyboard focus. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it appears without fading.
