### Contrast

On **Primary**, the resting label stays `color/text/primary` (`#1A1A1A`) — white on the light `action/primary` orange fill measures 1.86:1 and fails WCAG AA (needs 4.5:1 for normal text). On **hover** and **active** the fill darkens (`action/primary/hover` `#B15E10`, `action/primary/active` `#C6504E`), and the label switches to `color/text/inverse` (`#FFFFFF`): dark text on those fills is only ~3.7:1 and ~3.9:1, while white reaches ~4.7:1 and ~4.5:1. The same applies to **Text Dark BG**, which sits on an orange fill in its hover/active states.

### Focus

All variants show a 2px solid `color/border/focus` outline, offset 2px, on keyboard focus (`:focus-visible` — not on mouse click).

### Motion

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the hover lift (`translateY`) is dropped; only the background-color transition remains, so the button still gives feedback without motion.

### Semantics

Renders a native `<button>` — screen readers announce it as a button with its label automatically. Pass `disabled` rather than styling a non-interactive look onto an enabled element; `disabled` also removes it from the tab order.
