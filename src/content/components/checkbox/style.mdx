### Size & shape

The visible box is 26 × 26px with `radius/sm` (8px) and a 1.5px border (`icon/stroke`). It sits inside a 34px hit area — 4px of `space/3xs` padding on every side. The check mark is the `lucide/check` icon at 24 × 23px, centered in the box.

### States

| State | Fill | Border | Mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Empty | `#F3F0EB` (primary/100) | `#DFDFDF` (border/light) | none |
| Checked | `#C6504E` (action/primary-active) | `#C6504E` | white check |
| Error | `#F3F0EB` (primary/100) | `#CE574E` (feedback/error) | none |

### Added in code

Focus shows a 2px `border/focus` ring with a 2px offset. Disabled dims the box to `opacity/disabled` (40%). Neither is drawn in the Figma component.
