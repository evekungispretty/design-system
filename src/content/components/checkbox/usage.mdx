Checkbox is the basic building block for yes/no choices in forms and lists — agreeing to terms, ticking the options that apply, selecting rows to act on.

The component is just the box. It has no label of its own, so it is normally placed next to text inside a `label`. When the whole row should be the click target, and presented as a card, use [Checkbox Interactive](/components/checkbox-interactive) instead.

Three states are drawn in Figma: **empty**, **checked** and **error**. Hover and focus are handled in code; see the Style tab.
