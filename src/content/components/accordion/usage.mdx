Accordion hides secondary detail behind a heading so a long page stays scannable — in the source file, the selling points of a course and a refund comparison. A visitor opens only what they care about.

Each item is a white card. An optional blue number badge sits before the title, and a circular plus (closed) or minus (open) icon sits at the end. Clicking anywhere on the header row toggles the item.

Use `AccordionGroup` to stack several items. With `type="single"` opening one closes the others; with `type="multiple"` each opens independently. A lone Accordion works on its own too.

Items render **closed** on load. Figma draws both an open and a closed frame; the open one is there to show the layout and copy, not to be shipped.
