Course Card summarizes one course in a grid of courses. It carries just enough to choose from: an image, the course name, a sentence about it, and the categories it covers as [Pills](/components/pill).

Two audiences are drawn in Figma. **Kid** cards sit on the warm `#F3F0EB` surface with a red border and red pills; **Adult** cards are white with a purple border and purple pills. The card itself is not interactive — it is a layout for content. If the whole card should open the course, wrap it in a link in your page.

The card fills its container's width. In Figma it is drawn at 346px.
