Pagination Controls move through content split across pages — a course carousel, a list of videos. A round arrow button sits on each side of a compact indicator showing where you are.

**Dots** show a window of up to five dots with the current one dark. They are indicators only, and suit swipeable carousels. **Numbers** show up to five page numbers with the current one filled red, and each number is a button.

The window stays centered on the current page and slides as you move, clamping at the first and last pages. Figma draws only three positions per style (the first three of five); behavior at the ends of a long list follows the same rule but isn't drawn.
