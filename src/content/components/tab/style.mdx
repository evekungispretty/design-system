### Shape & spacing

Always `radius/pill` (999px), with `space/m` (24px) horizontal and `space/2xs` (8px) vertical padding. The label is `text/body-sm-bold` — step -1, weight 700, 26px line height — and never wraps.

### States

| State | Fill | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected | `#C6504E` (brand/red/900) | `#FFFFFF` (text/inverse) |
| Inactive | none | `#B3B3B3` (text/disabled) |

### Added in code

Inactive tabs darken to `text/secondary` on hover, and focus shows a 2px `border/focus` ring with a 2px offset. Neither is drawn in Figma.
