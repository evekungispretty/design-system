### Shape & spacing

Always `radius/pill` (999px), with 4px vertical / 16px horizontal padding regardless of color.

### Color

| Color | Fill | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| red | `#CE574E` | white |
| green | `#6DA979` | white |
| blue | `#6AA0CC` | white |
| purple | `#4F518C` | white |
| brown | `#B15E10` | white |
| white | `#FAF8F4` | `#1A1A1A` |

White is the only variant with visible depth — a 1px `#E8DCCF` border plus a soft inner shadow (`inset 4px 0 10px #E8DCCF`) that the five colored fills deliberately don't have.
